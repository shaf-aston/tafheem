Prototype · one Quran view

Read the whole surah, study any ayah where it stands

The "Read all of surah" view is now the only view. Tap any word, the ayah's end mark, or Study, and that ayah opens its word grammar, word joins and tafsir right under itself. Press play and it keeps going, ayah after ayah, lighting each word. Sample text is Al-Fātiḥah and Al-Baqarah 1–5; audio is simulated.

    Tap a word for its grammar · ← → change surah · J / K next and previous ayah · Space plays

    What changes in the code if you pick this

    Today → search or jump → AyahStudy card (one ayah) + separate SurahReader card (whole surah, 60vh inner scroll) → two players, two reading lines, two tafsir boxes.
    Proposed → search or jump → SurahReader scrolled to that ayah → ayah opens its study in place → one player, one reading line, one tafsir box.

    Keep as is

    • WordCard, TarkeebFigure, AyahTafsir: moved under the open ayah, unchanged inside.
    • useRecitation, useRecitedWord, useTranslation: called once by the reader instead of once per view.
    • Search box, root view, place picker: they now only choose where the reader opens.

    Merge into one

    • AyahText (AyahStudy) + GlossedAyah (SurahReader): both draw the reading line with hover gloss and lit word → one component.
    • QuranLookup's openSurah + at state → one {surah, ayah} place.
    • Reciter picker (only in AyahStudy today) → the bottom player, for every ayah.
    • AyahStudy body → an AyahStudyPanel rendered inside the open row.

    Likely redundant

    • AyahStudy's header: its own play button, ref, and the "Read all of surah" button.
    • SurahReader's Close button and Esc-to-close (nothing to close back to).
    • AyahList's 60vh nested scroll box → page scroll.
    • Per-row play + tafsir under every ayah → one player, tafsir only in the open ayah.
    • Rough size: AyahStudy 292 + SurahReader 332 lines → est. ~400 together.
