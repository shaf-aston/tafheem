“Press 1–8” as a moving hint

The line “Press 1 to 8 to open the tabs along the top” goes. Instead a faded keycap points at a tab and hops along: 1 → 2 → 3 … → 8. Hover the strip and it goes clear. Fewer words, the motion shows it.

A · Beneath the tabs, arrow points up

Sits in the gap under the strip, where the old line was. Closest to the eye on the page.

B · Above the tabs, arrow points down

Floats over the strip, like a tooltip. Keeps the page below completely clean.

C · A tiny key on every tab, lit one after another

No floating thing: each tab wears its number faintly; a wave lights 1 → 8 in turn. Hover shows all of them.
