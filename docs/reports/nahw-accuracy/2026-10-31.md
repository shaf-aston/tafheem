## 4. Not mistakes (my test was too narrow)

- أَعْطَيْتُ الْفَقِيرَ مَالًا, ظَنَنْتُ الْأَمْرَ سَهْلًا: engine says "first object / second object", finer than my "object".
- الْجَوُّ بَارِدٌ لَكِنَّ الشَّمْسَ سَاطِعَةٌ, زَيْدٌ أَبُوهُ كَرِيمٌ: I counted word positions wrong.
- إِنَّ فِي الْقَلْبِ نُورًا: parts are right, label differs.
- Quran verses use the Treebank's names (مُبْتَدَأٌ).
- جَاءَ الَّذِي نَجَحَ: الَّذِي as doer is right.

## 5. Fixes (PR 764, squash `73cfd21`)

Each fix is a rule from the book, not a sentence list. Each has a test with a sibling sentence it was not built from.

| # | Fix | Where | Sibling that now works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adjective shape فَعِل read when the ending is a sound plural or dual (new closed family `adjective_patterns`) | `sarf/noun_reader.py`, `closed_words.json` | حَزِنِينَ, تَعِبِينَ |
| 2 | A verb after a nasb particle (أَنْ، لَنْ، لِ) is the present one | `sarf/morphology.py` | لَنْ أَقْرَأَ, لِأَقْرَأَ |
| 3 | A bare plain noun in nasb after the doer is the object | `syntax/repair/subject_clash.py` | شرب الولد الماء |

Controls that must not change: `جاء الرجل الكريم` stays صفة · `أَنْ جَاءَ` keeps the past verb · `رَأَيْتُ الطُّلَّابَ` stays مفعول به.

### One regression caught on the way

The score gate (fresh set) fell 430 → 429. Cause: fix 3 made `حضر الطلاب أنفسهم` read أنفسهم as object. Fixed by skipping words on the book's emphasis list (`tawkeed` family). Guarded by tests for أنفسهم and `حضر الملك عينه`.
