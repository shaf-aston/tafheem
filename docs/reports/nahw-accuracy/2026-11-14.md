### One regression caught on the way

Fix 1 made أفهمْ show jazm. The jazm family's flat word list also holds مَن, so «مِنْهُ» became «حرف جزم». Fix 3 is the rule that closes it, with its own test.

## 4. Closed in the follow-up

Every item left open by the first PR is now a grammar rule with its own test in `tests/nahw/test_complex_rules.py`, and each test fails when its rule is reverted (mutation check).

- **k12 لولا + أنّ**:
  - the elided khabar «موجود» is drawn, and the answer is marked as left out (دلّ عليه ما قبله);
  - the «نا» card says اسم أنّ;
  - يفوتنا's object «نا» is on the card and in the tree.
- **Hidden doer**: a verb whose doer is a مصدر مؤول (k18 أعجبني أن تصدق) hides no هو.
- **Role chips**: ليت, ليس and أصبح show the word's own name (اسم ليت, خبر أصبح), not the family name.
- **Names**:
  - a hidden doer gives one pronoun, not «أنتَ أو هي»: هي only when a noun before it can be what it refers to, else أنتَ;
  - إمامه after إلا is بدل بعض من كل;
  - كليهما is ملحق بالمثنى.
- **Clause jobs**: the clauses of لمّا have their jobs (شرط, جواب لمّا); k11's jar hangs on its khabar لطيفًا (ظرف لغو), so no ثابتًا is drawn.
- **Card wording**: عندي names the pronoun «ي»; k14's tree no longer says «متعلق بـكم».
- **Open on master before this run**:
  - زيد مكرمٌ الطالباتِ: a nasb word after a tanween participle is its مفعول به, even in kasra (ـات);
  - رأيت النساء حاملاتٍ: a ـات plural's kasra is its nasb, so it can be حال;
  - مسرعات: an ـات plural the dictionary lacks is read as its singular, in the plural's number;
  - لولاكَ: the pronoun on لولا is a مبتدأ, not a verb's object;
  - مررت برافعي راياتهم: a sound plural that dropped its ن for إضافة.
- **Frontend**: tighter tree rows on a phone.

**Fresh review.** An independent reviewer read the whole diff and probed it. It found two real regressions and one latent bug, and all three are fixed with tests:
- A ـات kasra after a participle in jarr is a jarr too. So «رَأَيْتُ طَالِبَاتٍ مُجْتَهِدَاتٍ مَاهِرَاتٍ» stays صفة, not مفعول به; the kasra shows nasb only against a raf'.
- A subject that does a verb's work keeps the jar after it: «السَّفَرُ إِلَى مَكَّةَ مُمْتِعٌ» → متعلق بالسفر, «الْجَالِسُ فِي الدَّارِ مَسْرُورٌ» → متعلق بالجالس.
- An unsplit noun with ياء المتكلم is cut دين_+ي, never دي_+ني. نون الوقاية belongs to a verb.

Its fourth point, that a mask branch was dead, was disproved: a near-ties test fails without the branch. The full gate also caught one more case, «أُعْطِيَ اللَّاعِبُونَ مُكَافَآتٍ». The second object of a passive verb of giving now takes the ـات kasra as nasb too.

**Checked and kept:** k04 draws «في المسجد» on a ثابت صفة after the نكرة أحدًا. That is a valid book reading (شبه الجملة بعد النكرة صفة), not a bug.

**Known gaps, not fixed (each needs more than one sentence's evidence):**
- «مررت بالنساء حاملاتٍ» → بدل. After a jar the kasra fits a بدل too, so the sentence is ambiguous without context.
- «زيدٌ ضاربٌ ضربًا» → «–». A participle's مفعول مطلق is not named yet. This was true before this work.
- «الْحِفَاظُ عَلَى الْبِيئَةِ ضَرُورِيٌّ» hangs the jar on ضروري. الحفاظ (فِعَال, the masdar of فاعَل) is not on the masdar pattern list. Master gave ثابت, which was wrong too.
