# Nahw accuracy test, 2026-11-14: complex sentences

21 hard sentences (k01–k21, 152 words) in `backend/data/nahw_rules/complex_sentences.json`. A Sonnet sub-agent labelled every word blind: it did not see the engine's answer. Those labels are the key (`answer_key.json`, source `complex`). Then the same agent read the engine's cards and tree against its labels and listed 58 findings. I checked each word card and tree by hand, run locally, never on production.

## 3. Fixes, each a rule from the book

Every rule has a test in `tests/nahw/test_complex_rules.py`. Each test has a sibling sentence the rule must leave alone. Each piece of code was reverted once to confirm its test then fails (mutation check).

| # | Rule | Where | Sibling held |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A governor joined to و/ف (فلم، ولن) still governs the verb after it | `sarf/expects.py`, `expects.json` | ولن يذهبَ nasb |
| 2 | After لم only a verb can come, so أفهم is no elative before مِن | `sarf/morphology.py` | هو أفهمُ منه, زيد أعلم من عمرو stay خبر |
| 3 | مِنْ read as a preposition is not مَنْ of a condition, though both are on the jazm word list | `book.in_family`, `naming._family` | مَنْ يدرسْ ينجحْ stays اسم شرط جازم |
| 4 | A counted number (خمسةً منها) is the thing given, not حال | `facts/slot.py` | جاء الطلاب ثلاثةً ثلاثةً stays حال |
| 5 | A reason masdar (خوفًا) is مفعول لأجله, never a second object | `facts/sentence.py`, `facts/word.py` | تركت الباب مفتوحا keeps two objects |
| 6 | The ـات after a working participle is its object, kasra in place of fatha | `facts/slot.py` | زيد مكرمُ الطالباتِ stays مضاف إليه |
| 7 | A pronoun written on أنّ is its اسم: أنّنا اجتهدنا is the ناسخ, not أنْ | `syntax/particles.py` | أريد أن أذهب stays حرف نصب |
| 8 | A masdar is not a participle: its إضافة is معنوية, and the مُفاعَلة pattern is a masdar | `naming.py`, `closed_words.json` | قاتلوا مقاتلةً شديدةً → مفعول مطلق |
| 9 | اسم لا: a single noun or a dual is built on what its nasb would show, in the place of nasb; a مضاف stays معرب منصوب | `signs.py`, `card_wording/names.py`, `teacher.json` | لا رجلَ, لا قلمين built; لا طالبَ علمٍ منصوب |
| 10 | A raf' word after اسم لا/إنّ, where a صفة would be nasb, is the خبر | `repair/subject_clash.py` | لا رجلَ ظريفًا في الدار: ظريفًا stays صفة |
| 11 | A جار ومجرور hangs on a verb or what carries its sense, never on إنّ or لا: on a وصف khabar if there is one, else it is the khabar | `repair/khabar.py` (`jar_off_particle`) | لا رجل في الدار, إنّ زيدا في الدار → متعلق بـثابت |
| 12 | أنّ with its pair is one مصدر مؤول, taking the job of its place (مبتدأ after لولا) | `picture/places.py`, `picture/jobs.py` | علمت أنك ناجح → مفعول به; أريد أن أذهب unchanged |

### One regression caught on the way

Fix 1 made أفهمْ show jazm. The jazm family's flat word list also holds مَن, so «مِنْهُ» became «حرف جزم». Fix 3 is the rule that closes it, with its own test.
