# Nahw accuracy test, 2026-11-18 (hadith): إنما, nested sentences and a bug hunt

The work started with one hadith, «إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى…». Its card and tree had about ten faults. Each fault was traced to a grammar rule. Then a hunt on short hadith and textbook sentences tested the same rules on new sentences. Everything was run locally, never on production.

Every fix follows the same rules:
- It is a grammar rule, with a sibling sentence the rule must leave alone.
- It has a test, and putting the old code back makes that test fail (mutation check).
- A guard that no test needs is removed.

## 2. The إنما hadith

| Fault before | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| ما after إنّ read as إنّ's noun, or as a مبتدأ / خبر | ما on إنّ is كافة: it stops إنّ working, so the noun after it is a مبتدأ. In the tree ما is a particle (حرف), joined to إنّ as «كافة ومكفوفة» | `mask.py`, `naming.py`, `picture/units.py` |
| «لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى»: ما read as مجرور, or hung on إنّ | A jar said first is the khabar; the noun after it is the مبتدأ مؤخر | `repair/khabar.py` |
| «فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى…»: the jar read as a صفة, the answer split | A known اسم كان takes no صفة, so the jar after it is the خبر; the answer is one sentence, in محل جزم | `facts/sentence.py`, `picture/condition.py` |
| «فَإِنَّمَا الْعَدْلُ أَنْ يُسْمَعَ الطَّرَفَانِ»: أن + verb with no job | أن with its verb is a masdar. After a noun told of, it is that noun's khabar (خبر, خبر إن, خبر كان, خبر عسى…) | `repair/khabar.py`, `picture/jobs.py` |
| A relative after its verb (يَسْعَدُ مَنْ صَدَقَ) read as object | A verb has one doer: a relative after it is the doer while the verb has none, else its object | `facts/slot.py` |
| A jar after لولا's noun read as a second khabar | After لولا the khabar (موجود) is always left out, so the jar hangs on the noun before it | `picture/render.py`, `picture/unwritten.py` |
| ثُمَّ read as a second object of جَعَلَ | ثُمَّ is a حرف عطف, never an object | `facts/sentence.py` |

Tests: `tests/nahw/test_hadith_rules.py`.

## 5. Mutation check

Each rule was reverted one at a time. The tests had to fail.

| Killed | Mutants |
|---|---|
| ما الكافة and إنما | ما as a particle in the tree, the subject link on ما, the tag on ما |
| صلة | khabar after صلة: off, its gender check, its root check, its "مبتدأ before" check, its case check; naat check |
| inner sentences | the verb inside the sentence, a verbless sentence of three words, R7 |
| two known nouns | pass off, its order check, its ما نافية check, its stopped-إنّ check, its no-khabar shape, works-as-verb, ظرف, known first, one subject only; is_known with a written pronoun |
| no مبتدأ | nasb first word (`sentence_khabar`), nasb word before (`_inner_sentence`), توكيد |
| words | long vowel, stands_for, added shadda, منقوص (three mutants), منقوص never nasb, منقوص after a verb is raf', و as joiner, و before a case, رابط is third person, صلة number |
| picture | P4, P7, a صلة joined by و (`joined`), a sentence after a joining و is one unit |
| و + sentence = حال | the و hung on the verb (`_waw_on_verb`), its loop break |

Removed because no test failed without them: silah_kids, silah_known, said_below, last_sbj, stt, R10, R11, the whole pronoun-to-noun link.

Removed because it was wrong: `lone_described_noun` (made «الطَّالِبُ الْمُجْتَهِدُ» a sentence). Eleven older tests hold the phrase reading, so it broke them; the full suite caught it.

Picture: the fix «a و before a word in nasb is no جار» moved a hidden ثابت in two recorded Qur'an trees (15, 16), and the و-sentence unit boxed five more (Qur'an 5, 8, 38; rules 45, 47). Each was checked by eye and redrawn; nothing else changed.

Lesson: one removal (`joined` in `picture/jobs.py`) was checked against the role tests only, and broke a recorded tree (Qur'an replay 18). It was put back. A guard is now removed only after the picture replays pass too.
