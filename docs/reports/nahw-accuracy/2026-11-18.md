## 4. C. Bug hunt

A sub-agent wrote 40 fresh textbook sentences (149 words) and labelled them blind. The engine got all 149 right. Hand probes around the same rules found ten faults. Each fix is a rule with a test and a sibling it must leave alone, in `tests/nahw/test_complex_rules.py`.

| # | Sentence | Before | Rule | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ … (whole hadith) | ربي بدل | A raf' pronoun hung on a later لا is no part of its sentence; it is a مبتدأ, and the noun after it its khabar | `repair/khabar.py` (`before_la`) |
| 2 | اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي (short) | ربي مفعول به | A called noun takes no object | `repair/khabar.py` (`object_of_noun`) |
| 3 | هُوَ الْحَيُّ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ | الحي on لا | The khabar of لا never stands before it, so a word hung on it from before is the khabar of the noun before | `repair/khabar.py` (`before_la`) |
| 4 | أَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ | ما صفة | ما and من describe no noun (mawsul `mubham`); hung on a noun before a past verb, ما is ما المصدرية الظرفية | `facts/follows.py`, `particles.py` |
| 5 | فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ | ما «–» | The same, where the parser hung ما on the verb as neither its doer nor its object | `particles.py` (`_timed`) |
| 6 | رَأَيْتُ الرَّجُلَ مَنْ أَكْرَمْتُهُ | من مبتدأ | من and ما after a known noun are its بدل, never its صفة | `facts/follows.py` |
| 7 | سَرَّنِي الطَّالِبُ مَنْ نَجَحَ | two فاعل | A verb has one doer (Tasheel 2.4.1): a relative hung as a second doer, straight after the first, follows it | `repair/subject_clash.py` (`outer_doer`) |
| 8 | الْخَيْرُ مَا اخْتَارَهُ اللَّهُ | ما حرف | A particle is told of nothing: ما with a مبتدأ on it is the noun, its khabar, and the ه is its عائد | `facts/kind.py` (`negates`) |
| 9 | لَكُمُ الْخِيَارُ مَا دُمْتُمْ هُنَا | ما مجرور | A preposition's مجرور is its object; a word hung on it as a modifier is no second مجرور | `picture/jobs.py`, `roles.json` |
| 10 | زَيْدٌ مَا رَأَيْتُهُ | زيد «–» | ما is for what has no mind, so after a person's name, before a verb holding the ه back to it, ما negates and the verb is the khabar (rule replaced after review, section 5) | `repair/khabar.py` (`name_not_told_by_ma`) |

Siblings held, among others: «ما رأيته» and «الطالب ما فهم الدرس» keep ما النافية; «أكلت ما طبخت», «أخذت منه ما أعطاني» keep ما مفعول به; «جاء الرجل الذي رأيته» keeps صفة; «فرحت بما قلت» keeps مجرور; «يا رب أنت الكريم», «ذلك الكتاب لا ريب فيه» unchanged.

### Mutation check

Each new rule was reverted on its own, and its test failed every time. Guards that no test needed were removed rather than kept:
- a `stands_for` line for a noun after a detached pronoun;
- a «not a pronoun» guard on the indefinite-noun بدل rule;
- an `is_describing` guard on the ـات rule;
- an «opens the sentence» variant of the pronoun بدل rule.

## 5. Fresh review: five findings

A reviewer with no stake in the code ran the diff and master side by side on new sentences.

| # | Finding | Fix | Sibling tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `_timed` made ما ظرفية in «أعجبني الكتابُ ما قرأتُه», «سرّني الخبرُ ما سمعتُه» | A حرف has no pronoun returning to it, so a ـه on the verb makes ما the relative. Hung on a verb's doer or object straight before it, that relative is the noun's بدل | the three sentences → بدل; «فاتقوا الله ما استطعتم» stays ظرفية |
| 2 | ما read as khabar in «زيدٌ ما رأيتُه», «محمدٌ ما كتبه» | ما is for what has no mind, so a person's name is told of by no relative ما (new pass `name_not_told_by_ma`). Card and tree now both say ما النافية, the verb the khabar | «الخيرُ ما اختاره الله» keeps ما خبر |
| 3 | فِعَال as a masdar pattern made كِتَابًا مفعول مطلق and hung jars on الرجال، الجبال | Reverted | «كتب الطالب كتابًا مفيدًا» مفعول به; «الرجال في المسجد كثيرون» jar on كثيرون |
| 4 | A participle's same-root noun became مفعول مطلق («أنت كاتبٌ كتابًا جميلًا») | Came from #3; fixed by the revert | «زيد لابسٌ لباسًا جميلًا» مفعول به |
| 5 | Every ـات word left out of بدل («إلى الكلماتِ كلماتٍ طيباتٍ» → حال) | No change: in nasb, كلماتٍ طيباتٍ is a حال موطئة (a جامد noun with a صفة), so both readings stand. Listed for the multiple-reading work | – |

Also found while fixing: a ما the parser hung as a verb's only object («قرأ الطالبُ ما كتبه») was read as negation, hidden only because the tree's answer was copied over the card. Now ما in a verb's doer or object place is never negation.

## 6. Known gaps, not fixed

- «الْحِفَاظُ عَلَى الْبِيئَةِ ضَرُورِيٌّ»: the jar hangs on ضروري. فِعَال is the masdar of فاعَل, but it is also the shape of جامد nouns and plurals, so the pattern alone can't tell.
- «الكتابُ ما قرأتُه»: ما is read as the khabar (the relative). «The book, I did not read it» (ما النافية) is just as valid. Only the meaning decides.
- «قام زيدٌ ما قعد», «جاء زيدٌ ما تأخّر»: ما is read as ظرفية; ما النافية is likelier. Only the meaning decides.
- «الرجل حاملٌ حِمْلًا ثقيلًا»: حِمْل (the load) is read as the masdar (مفعول مطلق) because فِعْل is a masdar pattern.
- «الجهاد» is tagged a proper name by the dictionary, so it is not read as a masdar and its jar hangs on ثابت. Reading every name on a masdar pattern as a masdar would break هشام, زياد and الحسن.
- y040 «ساعيًا»: the key's حال is disputed among the books.
- «سلّم على الطلاب من حضر» → من مفعول به. The parser hangs من on the verb, not on الطلاب, and the verb has no object of its own to rule that out.
- The box over ما المصدرية الظرفية says «حرف», not «ظرف زمان». This is the same in every sentence with it, and was so before this work.
