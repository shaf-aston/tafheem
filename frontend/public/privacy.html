Tafheem privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. Applies to the Tafheem website (tafheem-app.vercel.app) and the Tafheem Android app, which shows the same website.

What Tafheem keeps

  • Your username, if you choose to make one. There is no password, email or phone number. Your username and the number of words you have learnt are shown in the ranking unless you switch that off in your profile, and to members of any circle you join.
  • Your learning progress: which quiz and practice answers you got right or wrong and how long they took, under your username, or, if you have none, under a random id this device made, which carries no name.
  • Reports you send about a question that looks wrong, kept to fix the question; without your name once your account is deleted.
  • Settings and recent searches, stored in your browser. With a username they are also kept on Tafheem's server, so they follow you to another device.
  • Sentences you ask about in Practice, and the questions and explanations made for them, kept on the server without your name, so the same sentence is answered at once next time. Hadith searches are kept, without your name or address, to improve search.
  • Pages you looked at, up to 400, saved in your browser so the app works offline.

Your microphone

Only when you press record (to recite from memory, or to speak into a search box), your recording is sent to Tafheem's server, which passes it to Groq's speech recognition to work out which words you said; if Groq cannot answer, Tafheem's own server does it. Tafheem does not save the recording. A short technical record of each check (times, which step answered, the words heard and the verse they were matched to; no audio and no username) is kept to fix problems, until newer records replace it.

AI answers

Some explanations are written by an AI service (Groq). When you ask for one, the Arabic or English text you asked about is sent to it; for a practice sentence, the list of words you have learnt is sent too. Your username is not sent. The answer is kept so it can be shown again. Colloquial phrases are spoken by Microsoft's speech service, which is sent only the phrase.

Recording studio

People invited to record words for Tafheem have their recordings kept and published with the app as the voice for those words.

Server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel and the server runs on Oracle Cloud. Like any website, the server records each request (time, page asked for, IP address, browser type; never what you typed into a search) to keep the service running and count usage. A separate security log records refused requests and account events (sign-up, log-in, deletion, ranking choice) with the username, IP address and time; it is deleted after 90 days. These logs are not sold or shared.

Some pages load files from other sites, which see your IP address: fonts from Google Fonts on the home page, and Qur'an recitation audio from everyayah.com, quran.com and qurancdn.com.

What Tafheem does not do

  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No account with email or phone, no contacts, no location.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's or site's storage removes everything kept on your device. To delete your username and everything saved under it, open your profile and choose Delete account. Reports you sent are kept without your name, and the security log keeps the record of the deletion for 90 days. Questions: email the address below.

Children

Tafheem is a study tool suitable for all ages and collects no contact details from anyone.
