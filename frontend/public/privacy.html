Tafheem privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026. Applies to the Tafheem website (tafheem-app.vercel.app) and the Tafheem Android app, which shows the same website.

What Tafheem keeps

  • Your username, if you choose to make one. There is no password, email or phone number.
  • Your learning progress: which quiz and practice answers you got right or wrong and how long they took, under your username, or, if you have none, under one shared guest record that carries no name.
  • Settings and recent searches, stored in your own browser on your device.

Your microphone

Only when you press record (for example to recite from memory), your recording is sent to Tafheem's server to work out which words you said. It is used for that check and not saved. A short technical record of the check (times and which step answered, no audio) is kept to fix problems.

AI answers

Some explanations are written by an AI service (Groq). When you ask for one, the Arabic text or question you asked about is sent to it. Your username is not sent.

Server logs

Like any website, the server records each request (time, page asked for, IP address, browser type) to keep the service running and count usage. These logs are not sold or shared.

What Tafheem does not do

  • No ads, no advertising trackers, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No account with email or phone, no contacts, no location.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's or site's storage removes what is kept on your device. To delete progress saved under your username, email the address below with your username.

Children

Tafheem is a study tool suitable for all ages and collects no contact details from anyone.
