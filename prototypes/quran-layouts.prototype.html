One Quran view, four layouts

Every layout is the whole surah, opened at 2:218 the way a search hit opens it. Switch layouts in the pinned bar; you keep your place. Then try it: tap a word, an ayah's end mark or Study, press play, change the reciter.

What's real here

  • Arabic: Uthmani text (King Fahd Complex, via quranenc), all ayahs of six surahs.
  • English: Saheeh International. Word glosses: quran.com word-by-word.
  • Word grammar, roots, tarkeeb and tafsir come from the app's own data. Here they are labelled slots.
  • Recitation is timed, not sound: an artifact cannot load the audio host.
